# Opening message to Sarah

Send it as an Instagram DM with the screen recording attached (the video carries it, so keep the text
short), or as an email via her site's contact form. Fill in the two [brackets] with something true.
One real detail is worth more than any compliment.

---

### Instagram DM

Hi Sarah 🤍

I've been following your work for a while now. [Something real: the episode, book or post that
landed for you, e.g. "your Coast to Coast episode on Atlantis stayed with me for days."] The way you
talk about the Higher Self, and about remembering who we really are, feels rare. It's grounded and
generous, and very you.

I run a small studio called LightStorm. We build cinematic, story-driven websites for artists and
teachers. Your Giza photo kept calling to me, so I made you something: a private preview of what your
site could feel like. We called it *The Hall of Records*. You begin at the pyramid, step through a
hidden passage, and arrive in a chamber with three doorways: your sessions, your courses and books, and
your events and trips to Egypt. It's all in one place, in your world. (The recording's attached. It's
only been seen by me.)

I'd love to build the full site for you as a trade. In return, my partner Sierra and I would each love a
session with you. We've both been wanting to do this work for a long time. I'd also ask for a small
"Site by LightStorm" credit, and to share it as a case study, only once you've seen it and said yes.

No pressure at all, and nothing goes anywhere without your blessing. If it resonates, I'd love to hop on
a short call.

With gratitude,
[Your name]
LightStorm · [link to trevworld.com as a past project]

### Shorter follow-up (if she hasn't seen it within about a week)

Hi Sarah, just floating this back up in case it got buried 🤍 I made a private preview of a website
concept for you (*The Hall of Records*, from your Giza photo). Happy to send it whenever you'd like to see it.

---

### Notes
- Lead with the video. On a phone, the 15–20 s recording is the pitch, and the words are just the frame.
- **Don't mention AI** unless she asks. If she does, say it plainly: her own photo is never altered by
  AI. The passage and the chamber are generated scenery, and she approves everything before it's used.
- Agree the trade in writing before you build: the scope (number of pages), what she provides (copy,
  photos, links), sessions for two, the credit wording, and case-study approval.
