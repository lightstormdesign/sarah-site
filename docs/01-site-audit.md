## Who she is, in her own positioning
- **"The Holistic Hypnotist"**: a Level 3 QHHT practitioner (Dolores Cannon's Quantum Healing
  Hypnosis Technique). Podcast bios also say she trained under Dolores Cannon and Dr. Brian Weiss.
- Her focus is past-life regression, the Higher Self, and "Atlantis, Avalon and the Secrets of the Sphinx".
- Her media includes Gaia, Coast to Coast AM, Far Out With Faust, Super Natural with Chad Sasaki, and the
  Ascension Retreat 2025 main stage ("Avalon, Atlantis and the Secrets to Ascension"). She has about 200k
  verified followers on Instagram, and Dolores Cannon's official account interacts with her posts.

## Tone
Warm, sincere and mystical. She speaks to seekers ("the deeper I go into Egypt, the more I realize…"),
leans on the Dolores Cannon lineage for credibility, and is adventurous and travel-led. The voice is
personal, not clinical. This suits a cinematic site: it should feel like a pilgrimage, not a booking form.

## What's weak (the case for the trade)
1. **Her offers are scattered across about six platforms:** Squarespace (on two different domains),
   Acuity, Teachable, Patreon, Amazon and YouTube. Nothing ties them into one journey, so a visitor
   from Gaia or Coast to Coast has no obvious first step.
2. **Navigation labels don't match the content.** /services is titled "Downloads" and /classes is
   "Heal with Hypnosis", so a visitor can't tell where sessions, courses or events live.
3. **There's no clear session offer.** The flagship service, a QHHT session with her, shows up only as a
   $5,000 retreat bundle. Anyone who wants a single session can't find a price, a process or what to expect.
4. **Events sit on a different domain** (a squarespace.com subdomain), which looks unfinished and
   splits her search presence.
5. **The site doesn't express her world.** Atlantis, Avalon, the Sphinx and Egypt are the most
   distinctive things about her, and her real Giza photos are striking. A stock template can't carry them.
6. **There's no single place for media credibility.** Gaia, Coast to Coast, Dolores Cannon and the
   Ascension main stage deserve one "As featured on" strip.

## How the Hall of Records answers this
Three doorways, one journey:
- **Sessions:** QHHT and past lives. The process, what to expect, the purple velvet portrait, and booking
  (Acuity embedded, not linked away).
- **Courses & Books:** Atlantis, Avalon and the Sphinx as three "records". Each links to Teachable or Amazon.
- **Events, Trips & Community:** Zoom live hypnosis, Egypt trips and retreats, Patreon Inner Circle.
