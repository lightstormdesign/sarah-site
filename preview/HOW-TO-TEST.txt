HALL OF RECORDS · PRIVATE PREVIEW · HOW TO TEST

On your computer
  Mac:      double-click start-mac.command  (first time: right-click > Open, then Open)
  Windows:  double-click start-windows.bat
  Your browser opens the preview. Tap "Begin the journey".

On your iPhone (the real test)
  1. Phone and computer on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Start the preview on the computer (above). The window shows "On your phone: http://...:8000".
  3. Type that address into Safari on the phone.
  4. For full screen with no bars: Share > Add to Home Screen, then open it from the home screen icon.
  5. Add ?auto to the address (e.g. http://192.168.1.20:8000/?auto) to start by itself for a recording.
  "Replay" is in the footer of the landing page.

Things to know
  - It needs Python 3 (built into most Macs; if the Mac asks to install developer tools, say yes).
  - If the phone can't connect, the computer's firewall may be blocking it: allow Python, or use the
    no-install option below.
  - No-install option: drag this whole folder onto app.netlify.com/drop to get a temporary link.
    That link is public to anyone who has it, so delete the site when you're done testing.
  - Double-clicking index.html also works on a computer, but the phone needs one of the options above.
  - Low Power Mode on: the loops show as still images (that's expected, the journey still runs).
