My Story

Sarah Breskman Cosme

Master Hypnotist · Level 3 QHHT Practitioner · Past-Life Regressionist · Author

I

A search for something more

Sarah earned her bachelor’s in Psychology at Northeastern University in Boston, then worked as a counselor in a halfway house for the mentally ill.

“I saw firsthand how the conventional treatment for the mentally ill with medication and talk therapy was not always effective, and I wished that there was something more that I could offer my clients.”

That wish led her to hypnotherapy: working with the subconscious mind to change limiting beliefs and unwanted behavior.

II

The path of the hypnotist

Sarah became a Master Hypnotist in 2009, then trained with Dr. Brian Weiss to become a certified Past-Life Regressionist.

From there she trained with Dolores Cannon’s daughter, Julia Cannon, in Dolores’s Quantum Healing Hypnosis Technique, and after years of dedication earned the coveted Level 3 practitioner status.

III

Teaching around the world

Today Sarah assists Julia Cannon in teaching QHHT around the world, in Egypt, Mt. Shasta, Peru and Miami, and has practised hypnosis and the healing arts since 2009.

What is QHHT?
